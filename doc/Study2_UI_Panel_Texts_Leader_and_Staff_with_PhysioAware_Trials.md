# Study 2 WoZ：Leader Trial Panels 与 Experimental Staff Episode Panels（可直接粘贴到 UI）

## 0. 使用说明

本文件提供两类 UI 提示板内容：

1. **Leader Trial Panel**：每个 trial 开始前给 leader 查看，说明本 trial 需要做什么，以及每个 episode 对应讨论的 item。
2. **Experimental Staff Episode Panel**：每个 episode 给 experimental staff 查看，说明该 episode 围绕 item 要做什么、讨论哪些点、以及每位 staff 的角色任务。

提示板不是逐句台词，而是用于保证每个 trial / episode 的讨论结构一致。  
Experimental staff 仍然应自然讨论，不需要背固定台词。

# Block 1：Speaking Context — Island Survival（荒岛求生）

## B1-T1 Leader Trial Panel

```text
Trial: Island Survival — Speaking Context
Duration: 300 seconds

Your role:
You are the leader. Please guide the discussion, explain or summarize the current item, and decide when to hand over the floor to members who may want to contribute.

Goal of this trial:
Discuss the first four island survival items and decide their relative importance.

Items in this trial:
1. Water filter
2. Knife
3. Fishing rod / fishing net
4. Flare gun

Episode-item structure:
Opening phase (0–40s): Water filter
Episode 1 (40–100s): Knife
Episode 2 (100–170s): Fishing rod / fishing net
Episode 3 (170–240s): Flare gun
Summary stage (240–300s): Summarize the four items

Please focus on:
- Which items are most important for survival on an island.
- Whether each item helps with water, food, shelter, safety, or rescue.
- Whether any member should be invited to add their opinion.
```

## B1-T1-O Staff Episode Panel — Opening Phase

```text
Time: 0–40s
Context: Speaking Context
Scenario: Island survival
Item in this episode:
- Water filter

Episode task:
Start the trial naturally. Let the leader introduce the island survival task and begin discussing whether the water filter should be prioritized.

Discussion focus:
- Is drinking water the most urgent survival need on an island?
- Can a water filter help if the group finds unsafe freshwater?
- Is it useful if only seawater is available?
- Should it be ranked above tools, food-gathering items, or rescue items?

Staff roles:
Member A: Briefly respond to the leader and help enter the discussion.
Member B: Briefly respond or agree/disagree naturally.
Member C: Briefly respond and confirm understanding.

Important:
Do not trigger a formal speaking-intention event in this phase.
Do not compete for the floor.
```

## B1-T1-E1 Staff Episode Panel — Clear Single Entry Request

```text
Time: 40–100s
Context: Leader speaking
Scenario: Island survival
Item in this episode:
- Knife

Episode task:
The leader is explaining or summarizing the importance of the knife. Member A has a useful additional point and should be treated as the main target.

Discussion focus:
- Can the knife be used to cut branches, prepare food, or make simple tools?
- Is it useful for safety, first aid, or building shelter?
- Does it become more valuable when combined with rope, fishing gear, or shelter items?
- Should it be ranked before or after the water filter?

Staff roles:
Member A: Target member. Prepare one clear supplementary point, such as “the knife has indirect value because it can help make other tools.” Wait for the leader to invite you. If invited, speak naturally.
Member B: Normal participant. Listen and give only short responses if needed.
Member C: Normal participant. Listen and give only short responses if needed.

Important:
Member A should not interrupt the leader. The event is successful if the leader invites A.
```

## B1-T1-E2 Staff Episode Panel — Competing Entry Requests

```text
Time: 100–170s
Context: Leader speaking
Scenario: Island survival
Item in this episode:
- Fishing rod / fishing net

Episode task:
The leader is discussing the fishing item. Two members have possible contributions, but Member B is the stronger target and Member C is a lower-priority competitor.

Discussion focus:
- Can this item solve long-term food needs?
- Does fishing require skill, time, and energy?
- Is food less urgent than water in the first few days?
- Is this item reliable enough to rank highly?

Staff roles:
Member A: Normal participant. Listen and do not join the competition.
Member B: Primary target. Prepare the main point, such as “food may not be the most urgent need at first, but fishing is important for longer survival.” If invited, speak naturally.
Member C: Secondary competitor. Prepare a weaker or more conditional point, such as “if there are few fish nearby, this item may be uncertain.” If invited, speak naturally but do not appear stronger than B.

Important:
Expected path: leader invites B.
If leader invites C first, this is a target mismatch.
If neither B nor C is invited, this is missed.
```

# Block 2：Listening Context — Desert Survival（沙漠求生）

## B2-T1 Leader Trial Panel

```text
Trial: Desert Survival — Listening Context
Duration: 300 seconds

Your role:
You are the leader. Please observe and coordinate the discussion. Let members discuss, notice who has not had a chance to speak, and invite members when appropriate.

Goal of this trial:
Discuss the first four desert survival items and keep the discussion balanced.

Items in this trial:
1. Cosmetic mirror
2. Top coat per person
3. Water per person
4. Flashlight

Episode-item structure:
Opening phase (0–40s): Cosmetic mirror
Episode 1 (40–100s): Top coat per person
Episode 2 (100–170s): Water per person
Episode 3 (170–240s): Flashlight
Summary stage (240–300s): Summarize the four items

Please focus on:
- Which items help survival in the desert.
- Whether the group should stay at the crash site or move.
- Whether any member has not had enough opportunity to speak.
```
